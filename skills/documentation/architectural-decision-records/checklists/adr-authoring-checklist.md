# Checklist: ADR Authoring Review

Before publishing or accepting an ADR:

- [ ] **Consequential Decision**: Does the decision have long-term architectural, maintenance, or security impact?
- [ ] **Context Explained**: Does the context articulate the concrete problem without prescribing the solution prematurely?
- [ ] **Alternatives Evaluated**: Are at least two alternative options or trade-offs mentioned?
- [ ] **Consequences Disclosed**: Does the ADR explicitly state negative trade-offs, operational burdens, or new dependencies accepted?
- [ ] **Status Set**: Is the status clearly marked (`Proposed` or `Accepted`)?
- [ ] **Standard Location**: Is the file committed under `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md`?
